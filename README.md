# Queens Beauty Booking

Build a premium, modern, mobile-first website for Queen’s Beauty Parlour, an at-home beauty service business in the Borananda/Hyderabad area.

Design: Elegant, premium but accessible. Use warm ivory (#F8F4EF), muted rose (#E9D8D0), espresso brown (#49352D), and white. Use elegant serif headings, clean sans-serif body text, generous whitespace, tasteful animations, and beautiful beauty-service imagery.

Create a complete homepage with:

Responsive navigation and a clear Book Now button

Elegant hero section with headline, short introduction, and booking CTA

Beauty service cards for makeup, hairstyling, skincare, and other beauty services

Why choose us section, without invented claims

Gallery using clearly temporary placeholder imagery

Simple booking/enquiry form

WhatsApp enquiry button

FAQ section

Contact section and footer

Booking: Let visitors select a service and enter their name, phone number, preferred date/time, locality/address, and optional notes. Include a WhatsApp enquiry option. Do not claim bookings are confirmed automatically. Do not pretend the form sends or saves data unless a working backend is configured.

Important rules:

Do not invent prices, reviews, ratings, qualifications, business hours, or service-area coverage.

Keep unknown details easy to update.

Use temporary placeholders for unconfirmed content.

Make the site responsive, accessible, fast, and production-oriented.

Use reusable components and clean code.

Do not add payments, login, or unnecessary complex features.

Do not deploy or connect external integrations yet.

First build the core homepage with polished styling and working front-end interactions. Keep the implementation simple to minimize credit usage.
